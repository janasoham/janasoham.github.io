\section*{Summary Statement}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\noindent
My research focuses on the theoretical and methodological aspects of high-dimensional statistics, with particular emphasis on mixture modeling, neural networks, distance-based estimators, and information-theoretic methods. I develop statistically optimal procedures for problems such as empirical Bayes estimation, clustering, nonparametric and distribution learning, and study their fundamental limits. I teach undergraduate probability and graduate-level machine learning courses, and actively mentor students in research.

\subsection*{Brief Overview of Undergraduate and Graduate Teaching}

\noindent I have taught undergraduate probability and graduate-level machine learning courses at the University of Notre Dame (since Fall 2024), and probability and statistical machine learning at Princeton University (2023). My courses range from foundational probability for undergraduates to graduate level courses and seminars on high-dimensional methods and machine learning.

\subsection*{Regular Courses Taught}

\noindent\textbf{University of Notre Dame}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0pt, label={}]
  \item \textbf{ACMS 30530: Introduction to Probability} \hfill Fa2024, Fa2025, Sp2026\\
  Typical enrollment: 30--60 students.\\
  Undergraduate course covering probability spaces, random variables, distributions, expectations, limit theorems, and introductory stochastic processes.

  \item \textbf{ACMS 70100: Machine Learning Methods for High-dimensional Data} \hfill Sp2026\\
  Typical enrollment: 6 -- 10 students.\\
  Graduate course covering statistical theory and methods for high-dimensional learning problems, including regularization, mixture models, and information-theoretic approaches.

  \item \textbf{ACMS 80870: Modern Machine Learning Techniques with Application} \hfill Sp2025\\
  Typical enrollment: 6 -- 10 students.\\
  Advanced graduate seminar covering modern machine learning theory and methodology with applications to data science problems.
  
  \item \textbf{ACMS-67695-07 Directed Master's Project} \hfill Sp2025\\
  Enrollment: 1 student.\\
  Application of high dimensional machine learning methods to biological data.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Princeton University}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0pt, label={}]
  \item \textbf{ORF 309/ENG 309/MAT 380: Probability and Stochastic Systems} \hfill Sp2023\\
  Typical enrollment: 50--100 students.\\
  Undergraduate/graduate course covering probability theory, Markov chains, and stochastic processes with engineering applications.

  \item \textbf{ORF 570: Statistical Machine Learning} \hfill Fa2023\\
  Typical enrollment: 20--40 students.\\
